Leadership

Fractional Engineering Leadership

Two levels of engagement, depending on how much ownership you need.

Fractional Delivery Manager

For teams running GenAI, automation, or payments vendor programs who need hands-on delivery governance but can't yet justify a full-time senior hire.

  • Vendor and SI delivery oversight — scope, timeline, quality
  • Escalation management between business, vendor, technology
  • Program health reporting to leadership and steering committees

Typically 2–3 days/week · 3–6 month engagements

Fractional Director

For firms that need senior-level ownership of a technology or delivery function — without a permanent leadership hire.

  • Vendor selection and contract/SOW structuring
  • Building and scaling a delivery function or GenAI practice
  • Executive-level stakeholder management

Typically 1–2 days/week · ongoing or project-based
