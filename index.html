Fractional engineering leadership

Two levels of engagement, depending on how much ownership you need.

Level 1

Fractional Delivery Manager

For teams running AI, automation, or payments vendor programs who need hands-on delivery governance but can't yet justify a full-time senior hire.

  • Vendor and AI delivery oversight: scope, timeline, quality
  • Escalation management between business, vendor, and technology
  • Program health reporting to leadership and steering committees
Typically 2–3 days/week3–6 month engagements

Fractional Director

For firms that need senior-level ownership of a technology or delivery function, without a permanent leadership hire.

  • Executive-level stakeholder management
Typically 1–2 days/weekOngoing or project-based
